» pilates reformer

Pilates Reformer uses a specialized spring-resistance machine to deliver a full-body workout that focuses on core strength, posture, flexibility, and controlled movement. Unlike mat Pilates, the Reformer adds resistance and support, making it suitable for all fitness levels — from rehabilitation to advanced training. Sessions are low-impact but incredibly effective, leaving you feeling stronger, longer, and more balanced.

» trx

TRX Suspension Training uses your own bodyweight and gravity to build strength, balance, coordination, flexibility, and core stability simultaneously. Developed by a US Navy SEAL, TRX is used by elite athletes and fitness beginners alike. The suspended straps make every exercise adjustable to your fitness level, making it one of the most versatile and efficient training methods available. Expect a serious full-body burn in every session.

» box fit

Box Fit is a non-contact boxing-inspired fitness class that combines punching combinations, footwork drills, and conditioning exercises into one explosive workout. You'll burn serious calories, improve your coordination and agility, and build functional upper-body and core strength — all without stepping into a ring. It's fast-paced, fun, and incredibly effective for both weight loss and cardiovascular fitness.
